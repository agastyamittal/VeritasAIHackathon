        What to fix

          Change the design

          Grab an AC unit or vent in VR to move it; the analysis follows its new room.

          Line up your lenses.

          Auto calculates the alignment for your phone and headset. Choose Manual to fine-tune it. Center the phone in the headset before checking the alignment target.

          Lens spacing
          Image centers
          View diameter
          Landscape screen

          Alignment and view size scale with your screen resolution. Lens correction uses a starting preset; Manual lets you refine it. Your saved manual fit is kept separately.

          Start with Lens spacing. If the house still looks doubled after alignment, reduce 3D depth. At 0%, both eyes show the same viewpoint. Keep settings that give you a clear, comfortable image.

          Your fit is saved on this phone when local storage is available.

          Step into the design.

          Drag to rotate the house. Scroll or pinch to zoom. On a phone, the ☰ menu holds views, uploads, cloud files, layers and hand tracking.

          Upload to phone saves a file to the cloud; open the menu on your phone and tap it under Cloud. STL and OBJ files open in 3D and VR.

          Phone VR opens two eye views with no buttons; you control it with hand signs held in front of the phone's rear camera for about half a second, and a clap. Hand tracking and the microphone start when you enter and stop when you leave (sound is analysed on the phone, never recorded). Thumbs up / down: up / down a floor. 1 finger: thermal. 2 fingers: airflow. 3 fingers: heat loss. 4 fingers (thumb tucked): architecture. Open hand held out: walk forward. Pinch: grab or select. Fist then open hand: go outside / back in. Clap: recenter the view. Leave VR with the headset's Back button, or Escape on a computer. The signs for floors and layers also work outside VR. Lens fit and Walk are in the menu under Phone VR.

          Keyboard: 1 cutaway, 2 exterior, R reset view, Escape close VR or panels.

          Airflow, thermal and heat-loss layers come from the simplified heat simulation, driven by Weather conditions; Analysis also shows an estimate from a model trained on EnergyPlus.
